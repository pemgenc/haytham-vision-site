About

Engineering a new way to see information.

Haytham VISION is a Turkish startup based in Ankara and London, developing a modular AI smart-glasses platform for both consumer and defence applications. We combine optical engineering, computer-generated holography, sensing, and AI-enabled information systems to make useful information more accessible through wearable displays.

Our platform integrates light sources, spatial light modulators (SLMs), optical paths, waveguides, and display architectures in a wearable, modular design. It is being developed to balance optical performance, comfort, and efficiency across a range of everyday and operational needs.

Technology

Compact optics. Modular hardware. Clear information.

The optical architecture combines an RGB laser-diode light engine with an spatial light modulators (SLM) micro-display. Light is coupled into a waveguide integrated with the lens, directing the image toward the wearer’s eye.

The platform is designed around a temple-integrated optical module, with LiDAR sensing, a camera, speaker, and waveguide optics.

Optical path

RGB lasers→ LCoS micro-display→ Optics→ Waveguide→ Eye
